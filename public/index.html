Evidence Helper: What to Collect

Easy, non-technical steps to gather bulletproof proof of online harassment, cyberstalking, or deepfakes.

🛡️ Everything stays on your computer. Nothing is uploaded in public.
💬

WhatsApp / Telegram / Chat Messages

  • Phone Number: Tap contact info at the top to display their real phone number or username handle, not just the saved name.
  • Full Context: Screenshot the whole message thread showing full date and time stamps.
  • Do Not Delete: Keep the conversation in your app until you have securely anchored screenshots here.
📸

Instagram / Facebook / X (Twitter)

  • Profile Page: Always screenshot the suspect's main profile showing their account handle (@username), bio, and profile link.
  • Web URL: If using a computer, include the browser address bar showing the full URL.
  • Direct Messages & Comments: Screenshot threats, abusive comments, and story tags.
🤖

Deepfakes / Morphing / AI Abuse

  • Save Original: Save the file in the highest available quality without compressing or cropping it.
  • Link Recording: Copy the exact web links or channel names where the media was shared.
  • Proactive Block: Use StopNCII.org or TakeItDown to hash and stop viral distribution across Meta, Reddit, and TikTok.
📞

Threatening Phone Calls & Voicemails

  • Call Log: Take screenshots of your phone’s incoming call history showing exact dates, times, and call duration.
  • Audio Recordings: Save audio recordings or voicemails directly into this vault.
  • 1930 Helpline: Note down your complaint token number if you report to the cyber portal.
